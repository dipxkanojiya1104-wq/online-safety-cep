YOUR DIGITAL TOOLKIT

Six habits for a safer online life.

Explore these simple habits and use them whenever you're online.

🔑
01 / PASSWORDS

Make passwords strong

Use long, unique passwords. Never share them with friends, and turn on two-step verification when available.

🕵️
02 / PRIVACY

Keep details private

Don't post your address, phone number, school details, live location, or private documents publicly.

🎣
03 / SCAMS

Spot suspicious links

Unexpected prizes, urgent messages, and strange links can be scams. Pause and check before clicking.

💬
04 / KINDNESS

Stop cyberbullying

Be respectful. Don't join in when someone is targeted. Save evidence, block the bully, and tell a trusted adult.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑
05 / CONTACTS

Choose who you trust

People online may not be who they claim to be. Never meet an online contact alone or without a trusted adult.

🚩
06 / GET HELP

Speak up early

If something feels wrong, stop responding and tell a parent, teacher, or another trusted adult. You won't be in trouble for asking for help.

SAFETY STARTS TOGETHER

A little guidance goes a long way.

Children learn best when they feel safe asking questions. Parents and educators can help by talking openly, setting age-appropriate boundaries, and modelling respectful online behaviour.

  • ✓ Talk regularly about online experiences.
  • ✓ Review privacy and app settings together.
  • ✓ Agree on what to do if something feels wrong.
